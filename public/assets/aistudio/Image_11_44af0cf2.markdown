## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an industrial-refined, contemporary artisan identity tailored for high-end woodworking facilities, custom joinery operations, and modern furniture fabrication. It bridges tactile craft with modern precision manufacturing and automated e-commerce workflows.

The brand persona is structured, trustworthy, grounded, and technically astute. The visual language utilizes an architectonic layout philosophy: crisp boundaries, structured metadata blocks, high-contrast operational status cues, and dense information hierarchies that perform reliably both in executive offices and on rugged shop-floor tablets.

Visual principles:
- **Engineered Substance:** Clean, monolithic structural layouts reminiscent of architectural working drawings and CAD interfaces.
- **Natural Tactility:** Warm, rich timber-derived hues anchored firmly by machined graphite and cold iron framing.
- **Factory-Floor Clarity:** High semantic legibility, deliberate borders, and explicit state indicators that eliminate ambiguity during manufacturing runs.

## Colors

The color palette translates the tangible materials of the wood shop—aged hardwood, raw timber, cast iron tooling, and machine oil—into an ergonomic, high-contrast software environment.

- **Primary (`#8B5A2B`):** Noble timber amber. Represents core brand identities, active production cycles, active selections, and key system anchors.
- **Secondary (`#2C3440`):** Machined graphite iron. Establishes institutional structure, sidebars, dense data table headers, and primary operational controls.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7D32`):** Forest foliage green. Used for quality control clearance, batch completion, wood grade certifications, and positive stock tolerances.
- **Neutral (`#1E232A`):** Deep carbon cast. Supplies maximum typographic contrast, high-density line work, and dark shop-floor surfaces.

Functional accents for routing and tracking:
- **Maintenance / Warning:** Burnished copper (`#D97706`) for blade changes, CNC maintenance stops, and scheduled calibrations.
- **Scrap / Rework / Critical:** Terracotta crimson (`#DC2626`) for scrap lumber logs, dimensional rejections, and stockouts.
- **Marketplace & Logistics:** Deep petroleum slate (`#1D4E89`) for marketplace synchronization signals (Amazon, Mercado Livre, Shopee, Elo7) and dispatch routes.

Surfaces maintain crisp, off-white lumber tones (`#F9FAFB` base, `#FFFFFF` raised cards) with low-reflectivity graphite borders to resist glare under shop fluorescent fixtures.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy separates high-level executive insight, dense manufacturing workflows, and precise shop metadata into distinct type treatments:

1. **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for structural rigidity and contemporary geometric balance. Conveys industrial precision in station titles, manufacturing metrics, and dashboard headers.
2. **Body & Controls (Inter):** Highly legible neutral grotesque with micro-metrics tuned for intense software utilities. Manages long orders, nesting manifests, material specifications, and input configurations.
3. **Data, Tags & Numerical Indicators (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced type family used for batch tags, moisture percentages, cubic meter measurements, CNC tool offsets, serial IDs, and marketplace order numbers (`MLB-XXXXXXXX`, `SHP-YYYYYYYY`). Tabular figures preserve vertical alignment throughout production runs and ledger tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an architectural 8pt rhythmic grid, providing maximum structure for high-density tabular views, kanban production pipelines, and multi-channel order feeds.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 40px safe outer margins. Accommodates multi-pane master-detail workflows (cutting plans alongside inventory queues).
  - **Tablet / Industrial Panel (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Tailored for touch targets on mounting arms at CNC and sanding stations.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 12px gutters and 16px outer margins. Collapses dense pipelines into linear priority runs, barcode scanners, and scrap registration forms.

Grid items conform strictly to functional density: operational tracking tables enforce compact cell padding (`space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal), while high-level analytical cards enforce `space-lg` padding to isolate KPIs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses precise architectural layering, micro-borders, and tactile physical delineation instead of heavy, muddy dropshadows:

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Canvas neutral background (`#F4F5F7` in light mode, `#161A1F` in dark mode).
- **Surface Layer (Level 1):** Solid white or graphite container tiles. Outlined with a razor-thin 1px border (`#E5E7EB` light, `#2A323D` dark) to guarantee edge separation under harsh dust-hood overhead lamps.
- **Raised & Draggable Cards (Level 2):** Production workflow job tiles in transit or drag states utilize an amber-tinted ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(139, 90, 43, 0.12), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Modals & Overlays (Level 3):** System dialogs, lumber cutting optimization modals, and emergency maintenance drawers. Bordered with high-contrast slate (`#1E232A`), accompanied by a translucent carbon scrim (`rgba(20, 24, 29, 0.65)`).
- **Tooling Depth:** Inactive zones remain stark and flat. Active work centers and interactive timeline stages illuminate with an interior 2px amber accent edge.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled, semi-squared corner treatment (Soft / Level 1). This reflects fine cabinetry craftsmanship—surfaces that have been planed and slightly chamfered to eliminate raw sharpness while retaining structural, rectilinear strength.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Operational Badges:** Standardized at `0.25rem` (4px). Keeps interface density high and maximizes screen utility.
- **Surface Cards and Table Containers:** Set to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px). Creates clear framing boundaries without appearing overly casual.
- **Sheet Overlays and Machine Dashboard Drawers:** Set to `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) along exposed corner radii.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted exclusively to binary status dots, active channel tags, and operational batch flags to separate metadata from actionable control buttons.

## Components

### Buttons & Quick Actions
- **Primary:** Warm Timber fill (`#8B5A2B`) with white text and a 4px chamfer radius. Active state darkens to `#704822`.
- **Secondary / Tooling:** Cast iron grey outline (`#2C3440`), 1px solid stroke, transparent fill, monospaced keyboard shortcuts bound to the right edge.
- **Destructive:** Rust terracotta fill (`#DC2626`) for logging scrap boards, canceling raw material imports, or stopping active runs.

### Batch & Lot Status Badges
- Constructed strictly using monospaced type (`label-sm`).
- Low-saturation tinted backgrounds with high-contrast solid borders (e.g., Quality Passed: `#E8F5E9` background, `#2E7D32` border and text).
- Includes structural prefixing (e.g., `LOT#8842-OAK`).

### Production Stage Timeline
- Linear progress visualization tracking 6 mandatory milestones: **Corte (Cut)**, **Usinagem (Machining)**, **Acabamento (Finishing)**, **QC (Quality Control)**, **Embalagem (Packaging)**, **Expedição (Dispatch)**.
- Completed stages render in deep forest green with filled circular nodes; in-progress stages pulse with a warm timber border and white core; queued stages remain muted slate with dashed connector lines.
- Each milestone node displays cycle time metrics below its label via `label-sm` monospace tokens.

### Artisan & Machine Allocation Cards
- Visual modular blocks assigning lead joiners and equipment (e.g., "5-Axis CNC Router", "Spray Booth A").
- Features operator avatar with craftsman specialization tag, active efficiency rating, and real-time load bar showing capacity utilization.

### Marketplace Channels
- Compact integration indicators with dedicated platform badges:
  - **Mercado Livre:** Warm golden yellow indicator with petroleum link tag.
  - **Shopee:** Deep burnt orange identifier.
  - **Amazon:** Industrial ink black with amber tracking chevron.
  - **Elo7:** Handcrafted turquoise marker.
- Displays synchronization status, pending order count, and automatic pack-slip generation alerts.

### Dense Manufacturing Tables
- Minimal cell height (36px–40px) to maximize data density.
- Persistent alternating row shading (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8F9FA`).
- Pinned batch identifiers and dimensional columns (`Thickness × Width × Length` in mm) utilizing tabular numbers.
- Integrated quick-filter bar containing stock species dropdowns (Freijó, Cumaru, Tauari, MDF), grain run direction, and marketplace fulfillment states.
